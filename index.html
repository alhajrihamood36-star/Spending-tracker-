💡 Save & invest in Oman

Turn the money that leaks out each month into savings that grow. Below are the main ways people in Oman put small amounts to work — with indicative figures.

How to start (Oman)

  • 1. Keep 3–6 months of expenses in bank savings first (your safety net).
  • 2. To buy Omani shares: open a brokerage account and an MCD investor number (Muscat Clearing & Depository).
  • 3. To buy global ETFs: open an account with an international broker available to Oman residents.
  • 4. Invest a fixed small amount every month (dollar-cost averaging) and let it compound.
